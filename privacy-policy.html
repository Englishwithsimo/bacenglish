This policy describes the English With Simo Android application, package com.englishwithsimo.app.

1. About the app and its operator

English With Simo is a free educational application for studying English, including Baccalaureate lessons, videos, interactive exercises and local writing practice.

English With Simo is operated by Mohamed Boujamaa. References to the operator in this policy mean Mohamed Boujamaa, not Google or YouTube.

Privacy contact: englishwithsimo@gmail.com.

2. Information processed by the app

You do not need to create an app account or provide your name, email address or payment details to use its educational features. The app does not include a student-submission service or an operator-run server for uploading exercise answers or essays.

The app processes your exercise responses, writing draft, word count, theme choice, lesson selections and searches to provide its features. Exercise answers, the writing draft and theme preference are stored locally as described below. Scores and word counts are calculated on the device. Searches run against the local lesson catalogue; the app does not implement a remote search service or saved search-history feature.

Free text can contain information you choose to type. Please avoid entering names, contact details or other private information into exercises or essays. The absence of required personal-information fields does not mean that all processing by third-party video services is anonymous.

3. Local exercise progress

The app saves exercise-field values and their corresponding answer references in private Android app storage so that responses can be restored. Grading occurs locally. These responses are not automatically uploaded to the operator or sent to YouTube by the exercise engine.

Use the Reset All button within an exercise section to clear that section's saved responses. Despite the button's name, it does not clear every unit. Changes are saved promptly, with duplicate or pending writes combined where practical. A response cannot be guaranteed saved if the app is terminated before it reaches storage or if storage fails.

4. Local writing drafts

Unit 1 writing practice stores its current essay draft in private Android app storage and restores it when you reopen the activity. It displays a live word count and a 100-word minimum. There is no name/email requirement, WhatsApp hand-off, essay submission URL or automatic essay transmission.

Use Clear Essay and confirm the dialog to remove the essay and its saved draft from the app. Cancelling keeps it. The writing activity does not log essay text, and Capacitor native payload logging is disabled in this version.

5. Copying to the clipboard

Copy Essay becomes available when the 100-word minimum is reached. It places the essay on Android's device clipboard only when you select that action. The app does not read your existing clipboard contents through its writing plugin or automatically send the copied essay elsewhere.

You choose where to paste or share a copy. The app marks its clipboard item as sensitive to limit content previews on supported Android versions, but this is not encryption or a guarantee against access. Your keyboard, clipboard manager, device settings or cross-device clipboard features may handle clipboard contents independently. Clear Essay, exercise reset, clearing app storage or uninstalling the app does not provide a way to erase copies you have placed outside the app. Use your device's clipboard controls and the receiving service's controls as applicable.

6. Theme, device storage and internet connectivity

The light/dark theme preference is kept in WebView local storage. Android WebView may also retain ordinary caches and third-party service storage, such as cookies, while displaying online content. App-controlled storage is configured to be excluded from Android cloud backup and device transfer. The app does not offer cloud synchronization of answers or drafts. These settings do not control independent clipboard, keyboard, Google-account or other external services.

The bundled lesson catalogue and exercises, including writing practice, can be used offline. The app checks network connectivity to enable online video features and display connection messages. This check is local; no separate operator connectivity-reporting service is implemented. Lesson lists use locally rendered video placeholders. Confirmed YouTube playback and its online resources require internet access and may use your mobile data allowance.

7. YouTube and other Google services

The app uses YouTube API Services through YouTube's privacy-enhanced embedded player at youtube-nocookie.com. Lesson lists display locally rendered Watch Video placeholders rather than remote YouTube thumbnails. Opening the app, browsing lesson lists or opening the video explanation does not initialize a YouTube player or request YouTube thumbnails through the app's preview feature. After you select Watch Video, the app explains that YouTube may receive information about your device and video activity and may display advertisements. You can choose Watch Video to continue or Cancel to keep using the available lesson content and exercises without loading that video. The choice is presented for each playback; it is not saved as blanket consent to third-party processing. Confirming Watch Video initializes the selected player and starts playback when permitted. Google/YouTube may then load video images and other online resources. Privacy Enhanced Mode does not prevent all tracking or eliminate third-party data collection.

Those requests disclose connection information, including your IP address, to the receiving service. Google/YouTube may process device information, browser or device identifiers, cookies or similar technologies, viewing interactions, playback diagnostics and advertising-related information for playback, security, measurement and, depending on settings and service rules, personalization or advertising. IP addresses may also be used to infer a general location. The app does not request Android location permission. Its local exercise and writing code does not attach your responses or draft to these requests.

Google's processing and retention are described by its own policies and may vary with your account, device, region and settings. No fixed third-party retention period or special data-processing agreement is promised here. Network and service processing may occur outside your country; this app does not provide a data-residency control.

8. Advertising and measurement

The app has no active website AdSense loader, separate native advertising SDK, operator analytics SDK or external course-payment flow in the reviewed version. However, the embedded YouTube experience can include advertisements and third-party measurement. Free does not mean ad-free or that no information leaves the device.

Earlier diagnostics of the standard embedded player recorded YouTube watch-time/event requests and Google/DoubleClick advertising-related requests. Testing of the current privacy-enhanced player also recorded playback and measurement requests after students chose to watch. These observations do not establish every request's server-side handling or mean that every video displays an ad. Cookie use and advertising-related processing may vary with the service, settings and session. Privacy Enhanced Mode is not a promise of no cookies, no tracking or an ad-free experience. No feature in the exercise or writing implementation sends your locally stored responses or essays to advertisers.

9. Retention and deletion

There is no timed expiry implemented for locally saved responses, drafts or the theme preference. They remain until you reset the relevant exercise, clear the essay, replace the preference, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app, subject to device storage behavior.

To remove all app-local progress, drafts, preferences and app-managed WebView storage, use Android Settings, open Apps, select English With Simo, and choose Storage / Storage & cache, then Clear storage / Clear data. Menu names vary by device. Clearing cache alone does not reset saved answers or drafts. Uninstalling removes the app's private storage.

These actions do not delete Google's server-side records, activity in a separate browser or YouTube app, or copies you pasted elsewhere. Use the relevant third party's privacy controls for those records. No app account exists to delete. The operator cannot remotely retrieve or erase a draft held only in your app's private local storage through this version's features.

10. Security and limitations

The app uses Android private app storage and requests its online resources using HTTPS. It does not add separate encryption for the saved answer or draft files, guarantee end-to-end encryption for every third-party operation, or promise absolute security. Device compromise, shared access, keyboard software, clipboard behavior and external services can affect privacy. Protect your device and avoid writing sensitive information.

The app does not request camera, microphone, contacts or location access for its educational features. Internet and network-state permissions support online videos and connectivity checks. This policy does not cover independent data processing by Android, Google Play or other software on your device.

11. Intended audience and children's privacy

The application is intended for users aged 13 and older, primarily in Morocco. Its content focuses on secondary-school/Baccalaureate English study. It is not intended for children under 13.

The app does not ask for date of birth, verify a student's age or implement a parental-consent flow. The intended age range is not an age-verification mechanism. Users under 18 should review the app's online features with a parent or guardian and follow applicable YouTube age and guardian requirements. The app does not claim child certification or verified parental consent. If you have a concern about a minor's information, contact Mohamed Boujamaa at englishwithsimo@gmail.com; use the on-device deletion controls for local drafts and responses. Google/YouTube may still process service data as described above.

13. Privacy rights, inquiries and deletion requests

Use the local deletion controls described above for data retained on your device. Depending on applicable law and the processing involved, you may have rights concerning access, correction, deletion, objection or complaints to an appropriate supervisory authority. Available rights and requirements vary by jurisdiction.

Mohamed Boujamaa receives privacy inquiries and deletion requests at englishwithsimo@gmail.com. The operator aims to acknowledge inquiries within 7 days and aims to resolve them within 30 days, subject to applicable legal requirements. These are operating commitments and targets, not guaranteed deadlines. Any shorter deadline required by applicable law or another binding obligation takes priority.

The operator will delete privacy correspondence when it is no longer needed, unless continued retention is justified for legal, security or legitimate administrative reasons. There is no fixed retention period promised for all correspondence; it depends on the request and any justified continuing need.

If you email the operator, the information you choose to include, such as your email address, name or request details, is provided to the operator and processed through the email services involved. This is separate from the app's local-only exercise and draft storage. Do not send your full essay or other sensitive information unless necessary to explain your request. Emailing a deletion request does not give the operator remote access to drafts stored only on your device; use the app and Android deletion controls for those copies. Requests about Google's processing should also be directed to Google's published privacy resources where appropriate.

The policy may need to change when the app's features, third-party integrations or applicable requirements change.
